# Key Business Questions by Module

## Customer Segmentation

1. Who are the key stakeholders at the leverage points along the patient journey (patients, patient advocacy groups, HCPs, organized customers, centers of excellence, payers)?
2. How do we define the customer segments that are most likely to adopt and deliver the highest value to the brand?
3. What specific behaviors need to change at the identified leverage points?

## Messaging Strategy

1. What parts of the selected behavior change, and business objectives do the messages need to support?
2. How is our Unified Brand Story supported by our data or claims? (i.e., does it effectively pull through the positioning)?
3. How do the drivers and barriers vs. the competitive set need to be addressed in messaging?
